##### Description:

- This plugin handles unwanted DTS stereo tracks and converts them to FLAC. FLAC can be somewhat commonly found on older releases to preserve original stereo track. 
---

##### Documentation:

For information on the available encoder settings:
- [FFmpeg - High Quality Audio Encoding](https://trac.ffmpeg.org/wiki/Encode/HighQualityAudio)
--- 

##### Config description:

Compression Level: Changes how aggressive compression is. Higher being more aggressive (smaller size, longer encodes), and smaller being less aggressive (larger size, faster encodes)
